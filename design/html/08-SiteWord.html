Learn a word

A Romanian word every month, and words our members teach each other.

Word of the month, October dor Say it like "door", with a rolled r

A deep longing for someone or something you love, often home. Romanians say it has no exact translation.

Use it in a sentence Mi-e dor de casă. I miss home.
This month's challenge Use "dor" once this week with a Romanian friend, and see if they notice.
