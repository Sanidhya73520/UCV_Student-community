How a club starts

  1. 1 Upvote Show which activities you'd join. No login needed.
  2. 2 Propose Log in and suggest a club, and say if you'd help run it.
  3. 3 Meet The International Relations Office meets the students who are interested.
  4. 4 Launch The club opens on this page, with its own events.
